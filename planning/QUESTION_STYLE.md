# Question Style Guide

## The real exam (reference: `pq1.jpg`, `pq2.jpg`)

The reference papers are University of Ibadan, Department of Computer Science, 2025/2026 second semester (CSC 572 Machine Learning). CSC 584 is expected to follow the same format:

- **Time allowed:** 2 hours. **Instruction:** Attempt any THREE (3) questions.
- Each question has parts **a, b, c** (sometimes **d, e**), each with marks in brackets. A full question totals about **20 marks**.
- Typical part types seen:
    - **Explain / define** — "Explain the meaning of X. Discuss A, B and C, giving one practical application of each." (9 marks)
    - **Differentiate** — "Differentiate between X and Y in terms of how they operate, how … are represented, and whether …" (6 marks) — i.e. comparison on *named criteria*.
    - **Describe with functions** — "Describe the architecture of … Explain the functions of …" (8 marks)
    - **State measures** — "State two measures for addressing each problem." (6 marks)
    - **Scenario (part c)** — a short real-world case, then sub-parts: "Identify the likely problem and explain your answer (2)", "State two measures (2)", "Explain why … is important (2)"; or "Recommend an appropriate technique for each objective and justify each recommendation (5)".
    - **Data/table question** — a small table is given and the student must identify problems and propose fixes.
    - **Briefly explain** a list of 3–4 terms, 2 marks each.

## How CSC 584 questions on this site are written

1. **Same shape.** Every sample exam question is `Na`, `Nb`, `Nc` (optional `Nd`) with marks, totalling ~20.
2. **Challenging.** At least half the marks in each question need *application, comparison or justification*, not just recall. Comparisons name the criteria. Lists ask for "explain" not just "list".
3. **Scenario part in every question.** Usually part (c), set in a believable (often Nigerian/African) context. The student must identify, classify, rate, recommend and **justify**.
4. **Tables and numbers where the course has them.** e.g. a DRG rating table to complete and interpret; a GIS dataset to classify as raster/vector; industry-growth figures to compare.

### Scenario types to use (by unit)

| Unit | Scenario ideas |
|---|---|
| 1 | A government wants to grow a local ICT industry — which segment (hardware/software/services/software+services) and why; identify which 1.14 factors are blocking an ICT project; advise on procurement policy |
| 2 | Choose between teleconferencing, video conferencing and tele-presence for an organisation and justify; identify gadgets for a rural bank/clinic |
| 3 | Describe a project; student rates each ITPOSMO dimension 0–10, totals, interprets with Table 3.1, names largest gaps and proposes actions; mid-implementation vs pre-hoc use |
| 4 | A ministry wants to measure poverty from mobile data — identify representativeness problems (n vs N, Rwanda), Lucas critique, recommend data fusion; identify which big-data characteristic a case shows |

| 6 | Classify e-gov services as G2C/G2B/G2G/G2E; pick raster vs vector for given data; recommend digital archiving approach and benefits; identify e-Health barriers in a rollout |
| 7 | Analyse an artifact's politics (Moses-style); apply macro/meso/micro to a tech-transfer case; apply UCD framework stages and TPB to a rural ICT intervention; INGO challenges |

## Model answers

- Under every question in `??? success "Model answer"`.
- Written the way a strong student would write in the exam: short intro sentence, then points, each point **bolded heading + 1–3 sentences of explanation**, and an example when the question asks for application.
- End with **Marking points** — how the marks would plausibly be allocated, e.g. "definition (1) + each technique explained (2 × 3) + each application (1 × 2)".
- For scenarios, show the reasoning (why this choice and not the alternatives).

## MCQs

- Purpose: *point out key facts and catch misunderstandings*, not to simulate the exam.
- 8–15 per unit, interactive (see `STRUCTURE.md`).
- Each has one correct answer and plausible distractors drawn from neighbouring concepts (e.g. G2B vs G2G, raster vs vector, Lucas critique vs Goodhart's law).
- Every MCQ has an explanation naming the section (e.g. "§3.4") and, where useful, why the tempting wrong option is wrong.

## Practice exercises

- Short-answer, compare/contrast tables to fill in, "classify these examples", "draw and label" (e.g. ITPOSMO framework), mini-calculations (DRG totals).
- Each with a reveal answer.
